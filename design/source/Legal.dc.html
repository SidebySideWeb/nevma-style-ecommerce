Duties and taxes

Tracking

You get an email with tracking and the numbers of your pieces when your order ships. Signed-in clients see the same in their account.

Limited does not mean final. You can return any piece within 14 days of delivery.

How to return

  • Tell us within 14 days from the day it arrives: from your account, or by writing to client care with your order number.
  • Send it back within 14 more days, unworn, with its label and numbered card.
  • We refund the piece and your original delivery cost to the same card through Viva, within 14 days of receiving it.

What happens to a returned piece

It goes back on sale with its number, and the edition counter goes up by one. We say so on the piece: “returned, checked, back in the edition”.

Exchanges

Because there are only five of each piece, we can’t promise another size. Return the piece, then buy the size you want if it’s still available.

Faulty pieces

Write to us with a photo. We cover return postage and refund or repair, whatever you prefer. Your rights under UK consumer law are not affected.
