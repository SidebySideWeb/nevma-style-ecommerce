The Viva handoff

Payment is the only step that leaves NEVMA Style. Shopify sends the shopper to Viva’s hosted page and Viva sends them back with the result.

1 · Shopify checkoutPay nowOrder created as pending payment. Stock is not taken yet.See checkout
2 · Viva Smart Checkout (hosted by Viva)Not designed by usWhat we can set in the Viva account: NEVMA Style logo, brand colour (#0B0B0C), merchant name, and which methods show (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, others Viva offers in the UK).[Confirm in the December test order]
3 · Back to ShopifyPaid → Thank you pageStock is taken, the edition counter drops on the site within seconds, the receipt email goes out.Not paid → checkout with a messageNothing charged, bag kept, pieces not held.
If the last piece sells while someone is on Viva’s pageShopify checks stock again when the payment comes back. If the piece is gone, the order is not completed and the payment is voided or refunded at once, with an email that says so. To test with Viva and Shopify before launch.
