NEVMA Style · The story

Limited by design

Not merchandise. Limited objects of identity, made in deliberate numbers and kept forever.

What we believe

Recognised without needing to announce itself.

NEVMA began as music: a London label releasing every month, and Moments, the nights built around it. Style is the third pillar. It carries the same sign off the stage and into everyday life, in pieces made so rarely that each one is known by its number.

Chapter I · The sign

A word you can wear

In Greek, nevma is a gesture, a nod, a sign given with the eyes. The first drop printed that definition as it stands in the dictionary, and it became the house signature: say little, mean it.

Chapter II · From the room

Born at the Moments

Some pieces appear first at a NEVMA Moment, in the room, for the people who were there. What is left goes online afterwards. The archive says where each one began.

How a piece is made

  1. 01The ideaOne sign, one story, often from a release or a Moment.
  2. 02Sampling[Workshop] cuts and finishes the samples until one is right.
  3. 03FiveFive pieces per colour are made, across sizes. Never more.
  4. 04The numberEach piece is numbered and signed by hand when it is packed.
  5. 05The archiveSold out is not the end: the piece stays here with its story.
