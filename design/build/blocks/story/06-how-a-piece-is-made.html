How a piece is made

  1. 01The ideaOne sign, one story, often from a release or a Moment.
  2. 02Sampling[Workshop] cuts and finishes the samples until one is right.
  3. 03FiveFive pieces per colour are made, across sizes. Never more.
  4. 04The numberEach piece is numbered and signed by hand when it is packed.
  5. 05The archiveSold out is not the end: the piece stays here with its story.
