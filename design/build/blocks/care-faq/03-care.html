Questions

The ones we hear most

Will a sold-out piece come back?

No. Each piece is made in five and never restocked. If someone returns one, it goes back on sale with its number.

Does my bag hold a piece?

No. Pieces are yours once payment is confirmed. The counter on each piece is live, so check out when you are ready.

Can I buy two of the same piece?

One of each piece per client, so more people can own one.

When is the next drop?

STYLE 002, Signal, opens on Friday 4 December at 18:00 GMT. Join the list on any piece page to get one email when it opens.

How do I pay?

You confirm delivery with us, then pay on Viva’s secure page by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

What is the number on my piece?

Pieces are numbered 1 to 5 in the order they are packed. Your number is on the label, the signed card and your order.
